The Story

Six chapters from the road to the tower.

Prince James Arthur walks through a rolling landscape, leaps over a fallen log, and reaches the castle gate where a great Giant blocks the way. The giant fights not with fists but with riddles — answer three correctly and he laughs and lets the prince pass. Get one wrong and you lose a heart; lose all three hearts and it is Game Over (until you bravely try again). Answer all three correctly and a Congratulations popup celebrates your wit before the prince rushes to Princess Claida Violet and they celebrate together — the kingdom is saved!

Behind the Magic

The computer-graphics techniques powering each version.

2D — Canvas API

Characters drawn from shapes, moved with transforms. A delta-time requestAnimationFrame loop keeps motion smooth; easing + gravity give the jump arc a natural feel. A particle system rains confetti during the celebration.

3D — Three.js

Characters built from primitive geometries in a scene graph. Ambient + directional lighting with shadows and fog give depth. OrbitControls let you rotate and zoom. Camera tweens into the reunion scene.

Shared Riddles

One riddles.js feeds both adventures — 7 riddles, 3 picked at random each playthrough, so Replay feels fresh. Correct answer → 🎉 popup. Wrong → lose a heart popup. All three right → celebration zoom.
